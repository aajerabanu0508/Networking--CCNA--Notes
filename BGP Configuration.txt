BGP Configuration
EBGP Example
AS 100                  AS 200
R1 ─────────────────── R2

R1:

R1(config)# router bgp 100
R1(config-router)# neighbor 10.0.0.2 remote-as 200

R2:

R2(config)# router bgp 200
R2(config-router)# neighbor 10.0.0.1 remote-as 100
IBGP Example
AS 100

R1 ───────── R2

R1:

R1(config)# router bgp 100
R1(config-router)# neighbor 10.0.0.2 remote-as 100

R2:

R2(config)# router bgp 100
R2(config-router)# neighbor 10.0.0.1 remote-as 100
Key Difference
EBGP:
neighbor X remote-as DIFFERENT-AS

IBGP:
neighbor X remote-as SAME-AS
